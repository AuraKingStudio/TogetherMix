How it works

  1. Create a room and pick the rules: who adds, play order, skip voting and more.
  2. Send the invite link. Friends open it and choose a nickname.
  3. Everyone adds their mix. Round-robin plays one track from each mix in turn.
  4. Anyone can play, pause, seek or vote to skip, depending on the rules.
