Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

No accounts

TogetherMix does not ask for an email address, a password or any other sign-in details.

What is stored in your browser

  • A random member ID, so the room knows which tracks you added and can let you remove them.
  • Your nickname, so friends see the name you chose.
  • If you created a room, a host key that proves you may change its settings.

You can clear this at any time from your browser's site data for this website.

What is stored on the server

Rooms, their settings, the list of tracks (YouTube video IDs and titles), and the nicknames people used in the room. Rooms with no activity for 30 days are deleted. The server does not store IP addresses beyond short-term rate limiting.

Videos

Videos are played through the official YouTube embedded player. TogetherMix does not download, store or re-host any video or audio. YouTube's own privacy policy and terms apply to playback.

Advertising

This site may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator. Replace this line with your contact email before launch.
